# Surface brief — landing page (`src/app/page.tsx`)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: examiners, district officers, universities, CSR partners and citizens seeing SDG Nexus first.
Job: understand in seconds that real local problems become verified, funded, finished work — and try a role.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a survey sheet of India's open problems. It refuses the category default of a dark hero, gradient
headline and three feature cards; the map and its live marks are the hero.

OWN-WORLD: Survey-sheet paper (pale grey-green, #EEF1EA), ink-blue linework (#1F4E8C), forest-green fields (#2F5D3A),
ochre contour accent (#B7791F), rust for alerts (#A4372A), ink text (#16212C). Hairline rules, graticule with degree
labels, a title block and legend box like a topo sheet, grid-reference labels for wayfinding. Anek Latin (condensed
widths) for display, Mukta for text. Lucide line icons only; no emoji, no glass, no glow.

STORY: A visitor sees where problems are being reported right now, reads how one moves from report to resolved (with
live counts per stage from the database), sees who does what, and signs in as any role in one click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5/12: sheet title block — name, one-line thesis, two actions (Report a problem — primary; Track a
report). Right 7/12: the India map in a framed sheet with graticule edge labels, district marks sized by reported
problems, a legend box. Real counts in the title block's "sheet data" line.

FORM: Survey sheet, first of seven derived candidates (survey sheet, railway board, sarkari file, India Post, election
ink, panchayat wall, NCERT textbook). seed: none (pinned) — user chose "Survey map sheet" in the direction question.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
