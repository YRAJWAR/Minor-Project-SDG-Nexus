# SDG Nexus — product context

**What it is.** A pan-India platform that turns local problems into verified, funded solutions. Citizens report a problem
in their district; the district officer verifies it; universities propose plans (team + milestones + budget) and the
officer selects one; CSR partners fund it; students and faculty deliver milestone by milestone; the officer verifies each
milestone and the funder releases that tranche; the citizen confirms the fix; students earn verifiable credentials. Every
step is written to a hash-chained public ledger.

**Who uses it, and where.**
- Citizens — on phones, often outdoors in daylight; short, plain-language flows.
- District officers — office desktops in daylight, many reports a day; scanability and the next action matter most.
- Faculty and students — laptops on campus; planning and proof submission.
- CSR partners — office desktops; where money goes and what it bought.
- Examiners and visitors — the public landing page and tracking pages.

**Truths that must not be invented.** Every number on screen comes from the database. Demo data is fictional and lives
only in `prisma/seed.ts`. No claims of real partners, users, government endorsement or outcomes.

**Brand commitments.** Pan-India, district by district. Visual world: Survey of India topographic sheet (chosen by the
user, Nov 2026). Indian typefaces from Ek Type (Anek Latin, Mukta). Light, daylight scenes.

**Modes.** Landing page: Persuade. Dashboards, forms, queues: Operate. Tracking page and credential verify: Read.
